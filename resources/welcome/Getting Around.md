# Getting Around

A short tour of the window, with the keys for a Mac and for Ubuntu.

## Sidebar

The sidebar shows the files and folders of this folder. Click a note to open it, drag files to move them, and right-click a file or folder to rename, move or duplicate it.

## Open Quickly

Press ⌘P on a Mac, Ctrl+P on Ubuntu, and type a few letters of a note's name to jump to it. With the box empty, it lists the notes you opened last.

## Find in Folder

Press ⇧⌘F on a Mac, Shift+Ctrl+F on Ubuntu, to search the text of every note in the folder, and replace it if you like.

## Outline

Press ⌥⌘O on a Mac, Alt+Ctrl+O on Ubuntu, to show the outline: the headings of the note you are reading. Click one to jump there.

## Backlinks

Under the outline, Backlinks lists the notes that link to this one. Open it now: Welcome to MarkdownMate is there, because it links here.

## Tags

The sidebar's Tags section lists every tag in your notes. Open a tag to see the notes that have it.

## Preview and Side by side

Press ⌘1, ⌘2 or ⌘3 on a Mac, Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 or Ctrl+3 on Ubuntu, to show the editor, the preview, or both side by side.

## Export

Turn a note into a PDF, a web page or a Word document with the Export items (in the File menu on a Mac, the main menu on Ubuntu).
